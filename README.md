# StardewValley

**StardewValley** is a large-scale Fabric mod for **Minecraft 1.21.11** that brings the charm and core gameplay of *Stardew Valley* into Minecraft. It restores most of Stardew Valley's signature mechanics, letting you build a farm, master skills, and live the valley life right in your world.

### Farming & Crops
- Dozens of Stardew Valley crops (parsnips, melons, corn, beets, garlic, kale, hops, grapes and more) with multi-stage growth models and seed items.
- Crop quality tiers (normal / silver / gold) and a farming level system.
- Tools: hoes, watering cans, pickaxes, and Stardew-style farmland; sprinklers, fertilizer and grass starter for a productive farm.

### Skills & Progression
- Five skill lines — Farming, Foraging, Mining, Fishing, Combat — each with its own level manager and unlockable perks.
- Mastery and skill-selection UI, skill books and buff effects tied to skill progress.

### Fishing
- A custom fishing rod with a real-time **fishing minigame**, custom fish behavior/animations, and over 40 fish species with seasonal availability.
- Bait, tackles, crab pots, treasure loot, and per-fish difficulty settings.

### Valley Life
- Villager shops from the original game: Robin, Marnie, Willy, Gus, Harvey, Marlon, Sandy, Krobus, the Dwarf, the Desert & Island Traders, the Traveling Cart, and a Bookseller.
- Museum donations, Community Center **bundles**, and a shipping box economy with gold currency and a clock HUD.
- Cooking system with classic recipes (pizza, omelet, sashimi, chowder, cookies and more).

### Animals & Enemies
- Animal friendship, golden animal crackers, chicken eggs, pig truffles, and rabbit feet.
- New weapons, daggers, bombs, and a slingshot; revamped monster drops and combat rewards.

### World & Utility
- Seasons and weather (rain, storms, green rain) with matching foraging loot and luck modifiers.
- Warp totems, rain totems, and a guide book with in-game help.
- Custom equipment slots (rings, shoes, backpacks, trash cans) and stackable item support.
